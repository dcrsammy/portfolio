Questions guests ask

What is the caution fee?

A refundable deposit that covers any damage. We refund it in full within 24 hours of checkout once the apartment is checked.

Are parties allowed?

Small gatherings are fine with the host's approval. Loud parties are not allowed, because our neighbours live in the estate too.

What do I need to check in?

A valid ID for the main guest. We ask for it on WhatsApp before we send your door code.

What time is check-in and checkout?

Check in from 2pm and check out by 12 noon. Early check-in or late checkout can be arranged when the calendar allows.

Can I cancel?

Cancel up to 7 days before check-in for a full refund of your deposit. December bookings are non-refundable but can be moved to other dates.

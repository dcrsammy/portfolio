Background

Builder. Designer. Founder.

I'm a Lagos-based full-stack developer with a bias for execution. I've shipped production-grade platforms, built and deployed e-commerce systems, and handled everything from database migrations to brand identity — sometimes on the same project.

Before Lagos, I was based in China — retained developer for Oriflame Trading Company and Eternal Energy Trading Company, and a student at Sanming University and Nanjing University of Technology. That time sharpened a particular interest: the gap between what Nigerian consumers need and what currently exists for them.

Most of my work now lives at that intersection — tech platforms built for the Nigerian market, with the quality bar of anywhere else.
